# pending

Tend the frictions scoped to your reach: `pending` lists them (the database filters
by your real lineage), resolve the operational ones with your mounts (`resolve`,
with a `resolvedThrough` trace), hand off within reach (`rescope`), leave the
structural ones for the root drain. Journal the pass. Only when the human asks.
