# Enterprise tier price floor

Enterprise engagements are never quoted below 20k. Discounts beyond 10% require the
sales owner's sign-off; anything beyond 20% is a governance decision, not a sales one.

Rationale: three deals in Q2 eroded margin chasing volume; the floor protects the
positioning the content purpose builds.
